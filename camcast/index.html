About CamCast

CamCast shows public, intentionally shared camera feeds: official open-data road and city cameras worldwide (Caltrans, TfL London, Fintraffic, Hong Kong TD, NZTA, PennDOT 511PA) and owner-published YouTube 24/7 live cams, mountain/weather road cams, NOAA GOES satellite imagery and NASA's public live stream.

No Flock / ALPR (license plate) cameras, no private or unsecured cameras, nothing behind a login.

Feeds belong to their publishers (see attribution on each). Availability depends on the source.
